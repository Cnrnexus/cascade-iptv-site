Privacy Policy

Cascade IPTV for Windows, Android, Android TV and Fire TV. Effective November 23, 2026.

Cascade IPTV is a media player published by Cascade Player Labs, a product line of CNR Nexus Consulting Inc. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. We operate no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see what you watch.

Information stored on your device

Windows: the playlist addresses and sign-in details you enter are saved on your device and encrypted with Windows data protection, tied to your Windows account. The app also keeps settings, favourites, viewing progress, a cache of your channel list and programme guide, and small diagnostic logs in the .cascade folder of your Windows user profile. Recordings you make are saved to your Videos folder.

Android, Android TV and Fire TV: playlist addresses and sign-in details are saved in the app's private storage and encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are excluded from cloud backups. The app also keeps settings, favourites, viewing progress, a parental PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) and a cache of your channel list, programme guide and film and series titles. The cache does not contain your sign-in details. Recordings you make are saved on the device.

Cached channel data can include stream addresses issued by your provider. None of this information is sent to us.

Information sent over the internet

The app connects only to the servers named in the playlists you add, to download the playlist, the programme guide, channel logos and the streams you play. Your sign-in details are sent to your own provider for that purpose, in the way that provider requires. We have no control over those servers, and your provider's own privacy policy applies to them.

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from: the Microsoft Store, Google Play or the Amazon Appstore. The app receives only a confirmation that the unlock was purchased, and remembers that on the device. We do not receive your payment details.

Casting (Android, Google Play version): when you cast to a Chromecast or Google TV, the app uses Google's Cast software to find devices on your local network and send them the stream you chose. Google's privacy policy applies to that software. While casting a live channel, the app may relay the stream to your TV over your local network only.

Deleting your data

Remove a playlist in Setup to delete its saved details. On Windows, to remove everything, uninstall the app and delete the .cascade folder in your user profile. On Android and Fire TV, uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
