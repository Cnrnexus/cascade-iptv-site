Privacy Policy

Cascade IPTV for Windows. Effective November 13, 2026.

Cascade IPTV is a media player published by Cascade Player Labs, a product line of CNR Nexus Consulting Inc. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. We operate no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see what you watch.

Information stored on your device

The playlist addresses and sign-in details you enter are saved on your device and encrypted with Windows data protection, tied to your Windows account. The app also keeps settings, favourites, viewing progress, a cache of your channel list and programme guide, and small diagnostic logs in the .cascade folder of your Windows user profile. Cached channel data can include stream addresses issued by your provider. These files stay on your device and are never sent to us. Recordings you make are saved to your Videos folder.

Information sent over the internet

The app connects only to the servers named in the playlists you add, to download the playlist, the programme guide, channel logos and the streams you play. Your sign-in details are sent to your own provider for that purpose, in the way that provider requires. We have no control over those servers, and your provider's own privacy policy applies to them.

Purchases made through the Microsoft Store are handled by Microsoft. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Remove a playlist in Setup to delete its saved details. To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete the .cascade folder in your user profile.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
