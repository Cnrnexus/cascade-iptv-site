Help with Cascade IPTV for Windows.

Getting started

Open Setup, choose Add playlist, and enter the M3U link or Xtream Codes details from your provider. Cascade IPTV includes no channels; you need a playlist from a provider you are entitled to use.

Try it with free test streams

To see how the app works before adding your own provider, add this playlist of freely licensed sample streams:

https://cnrnexus.github.io/cascade-iptv-site/test/cascade-test.m3u
